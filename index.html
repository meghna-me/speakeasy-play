Know your bartender

She has a rule tonight. She will not tell you what it is — she will just take what she likes and slide the rest back.

She only ever cares about four things: how many drinks you order, their colours, their sizes, and the order they come in. That's the whole of it. Nothing about the day, nothing about you, nothing she hasn't already shown you.

So a house rule sounds like

  • “There are exactly three drinks.”
  • “The smallest glass comes last.”
  • “There's more than one colour in it.”

Not tonight's — she'd never make it that easy.

How a night goes

  1. Order a round. Anything. She takes it or she doesn't, and either way you've learned something.
  2. Then call it first. Say what she'll do before you ask. Being wrong is the good part — that's where the rule shows.
  3. Say you've got her. She'll put four rounds in front of you. Read her right on all four and the night is yours.
